## Why

A task closed from the calendar stays on its day, ticked, and a task closed on the board stays
in the calendar too. The calendar is a plan of what is still to do, so finished work only
crowds it.

## What Changes

- The task calendar no longer draw a task in the Готово or archived stage.
  Closing a task leaves its card at once; reopening it on the board brings it back.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `task-calendar`: finished tasks are not drawn.

## Impact

- Web: `widgets/task-calendar`.
