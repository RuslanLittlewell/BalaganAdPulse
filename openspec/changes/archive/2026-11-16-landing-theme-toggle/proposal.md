## Why

The landing follows the visitor's system scheme and offers no way to try the other one, though
its light and dark looks differ a lot.

## What Changes

- A control at the header's left edge switches between the light and the dark scheme.
- The choice is stored in the browser and applied before the first paint on every landing page.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `public-landing`: the visitor can choose the colour scheme.

## Impact

- `apps/landing`: the header, the base layout's head and the colour tokens.
