## Why

The connections section opens with a heading and goes straight into the step-by-step guides, so a
visitor has to read to learn which services BalaganBI works with. A short visual overview of the
integrations, connected and planned, answers that at a glance.

## What Changes

- The connections section opens with a heading and a bento of three live diagrams, following the
  layout of a features section the user picked: accounts flowing through BalaganBI into reports, a
  hub of integrations, and a 30-day chart that can be scrubbed.
- The hub shows Meta Ads, Google Ads, Google Search Console and Telegram as connected, and Яндекс
  Директ and TikTok Ads as coming soon. The separate «Скоро» card goes away; its notes stay.
- The connection guides and the Google data statement stay below, unchanged.
- The section carries a fine hatched background across the page's width.
