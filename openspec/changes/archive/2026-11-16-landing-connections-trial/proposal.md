## Why

A visitor who has just read how accounts connect is ready to try, but the connections section
offers no next step; the trial action exists only at the top of the page.

## What Changes

- The connections section's heading gets the free-trial action and the one-month note, the same
  as the opening.
- The trial action becomes one shared component, so both places look and behave alike.
