## Why

Account settings should be the first view when opening the profile dialog.

## What Changes

- Show Account before General and select Account when opening the dialog.
- Keep each tab's settings and save behavior in place.
- No breaking changes.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `profile-preferences`: Account is the first and initially selected tab.

## Impact

The profile dialog, its behavior tests and the profile preferences specification change.
