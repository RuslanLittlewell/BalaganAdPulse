## Why

Tagging in a comment offers only the task's participants, its creator and the admins, so on a
task with nobody named only the admins can be tagged. A comment is often how a colleague is
pulled into a task.

## What Changes

- Writing `@` in a comment offers every active employee of the organization, not only the
  people already on the task.
- A client's person is still offered only on a task shared with the client that names them.
