## Why

The opening puts the heading and a small dashboard imitation side by side, so neither has room and
the visitor gets no next step. A centred opening with a clear action and a large imitation of the
dashboard shows the product before the first scroll.

## What Changes

- The opening is centred: the handwritten signature, a short note linking to the connection
  guides, the heading, the lead text, and two actions, signing in and a look at the modules.
- Below them a large dashboard imitation, lit from behind, takes the full width of the content.
- The imitation gains the app's navigation, a header with the period and who is online, and the
  month's totals with their trend, beside the KPI gauge and the projects it already had.
- The second lead paragraph about KPI colours leaves the opening; the gauge's legend and the
  features section say the same.
