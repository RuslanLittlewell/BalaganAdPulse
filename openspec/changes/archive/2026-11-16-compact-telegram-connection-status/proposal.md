## Why

The connected Telegram state needs only a green indicator and the linked username, rather than usage instructions.

## What Changes

- Show a green dot and the linked Telegram username.
- Keep the connected status available to screen readers without displaying the word.
- Remove the unused connected-state help copy from both dictionaries.
- Preserve connection and notification behavior. No breaking changes.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This is a presentation change to the existing connected state.

## Impact

The Telegram section, its dictionaries and its existing text expectations change.
