## Why

General settings should show their controls without the explanatory text block beneath them.

## What Changes

- Remove the language reload and planned feature explanations from General.
- Remove accessibility references and dictionary entries for the deleted text.
- Keep preference controls and saving behavior unchanged.
- No breaking changes.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `profile-preferences`: Remove the requirement to display a planned feature explanation.

## Impact

The profile dialog, its dictionaries and the profile preferences specification change.
