## Why

The opening asks a visitor to sign in or to look at the modules, but most visitors have no account
yet. For a Russian-speaking agency the expected first step is a free trial. The dashboard
imitation also sits in a narrower box than the content and is laid out differently from the
dashboard it imitates.

## What Changes

- The opening's main action becomes Попробовать бесплатно, with a note that the trial lasts 14
  days and includes CRM, tasks and reports; signing in stays as the second action.
- The trial action leads to the contact form in the footer with the new topic Пробный период
  chosen. Registration still needs an invitation, so no sign-up form appears.
- The dashboard imitation spans the full width of the content and follows the dashboard's layout:
  header with the period, the month's totals, the KPI gauge beside the spend by channel, and the
  projects table below.
