## Why

The module imitations are small cards a few hundred pixels tall, so they read as illustrations,
not as the product. Shown as desktop screenshots of the app, they tell the visitor what they will
actually work in.

## What Changes

- Each module imitation becomes a desktop screen: a 16:10 window laid out at 1280 by 800 pixels
  with the app's address bar, its navigation with the module chosen, and the module's page, scaled
  down whole to the width it has.
- The pages follow the app: the dashboard as in the opening, a project with its accounts, KPI and
  campaigns, the task board with its seven columns, the CRM board, a monthly report next to the
  list of reports, and the team with roles and the activity log.
- In the deck the imitation takes two thirds of the width instead of three fifths.
- Sections no longer stretch to the screen's height: a short section left a screen of empty space
  before the next one. They keep spanning the page without a box.
